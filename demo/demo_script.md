# Demo script (2–3 min)

1. **0:00–0:20 — The story.** Student, 11 pm, Valencia St → Castro. A standard maps app sends her through Dolores Park in the dark.
2. **0:20–1:20 — Fast vs safe route.** Score, factor breakdown with data reliability, plain-language explanation.
3. **1:20–2:00 — Walk together.** Matched with 2 students going the same way, lit and open meeting point, safeguards.
4. **2:00–2:20 — Human in the loop.** A user report changes a segment's score and the route updates live.
5. **2:20–2:40 — Our judgment calls.** Crime data down-weighted and filtered (bias), AI never computes routes or invents facts, safeguards for meeting strangers.
6. **2:40–3:00 — Limits, who pays (universities, cities, campus safety), next steps.**
